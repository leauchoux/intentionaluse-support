Pause before opening.
Use what you need.

Choose what to protect and reflect on your intention before opening an app. When access is needed, set a short allowance of 5, 10, or 15 minutes.

MomenTap is designed for people who want to manage their own smartphone habits.

App support (Korean)Privacy policy

Personal choice comes first

People choose what to protect and how long to allow access. The app creates a small moment to consider that choice.

Choice history stays on the device

No account or sign-in is required. Optional choice history is off by default and, when enabled, is kept on the device for up to 90 days.
